Learned at the Source

I'm Pierre LeBlanc, founder of Tyrelitic Data Solutions. I've spent 25 years inside the tire and automotive industry — not watching it from the outside, but living in the data, the pricing decisions, and the software systems that keep dealerships running.

My journey started at Costar itself, headquartered in Sherwood Park, Alberta. That means I didn't just learn this software — I learned it at the source, alongside the people who built it.
